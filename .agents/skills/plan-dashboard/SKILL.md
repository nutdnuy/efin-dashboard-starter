# Plan a dashboard together

Start from the learner's question and BRIEF.md. Ask what decision the dashboard
should make easier; do not preselect asset classes, metrics, data, framework or
visual style. Continue from known answers instead of repeating the interview.

Use **ask-questions** for unresolved choices and **define-terms** when investing
words need a shared meaning. Ask one topic at a time in plain language, offering
concrete options when useful. Research facts yourself with **find-information**
instead of asking the learner to diagnose technical details.

Record the agreed purpose, first useful version, data availability, device and
visual preferences in BRIEF.md. Keep unknowns explicitly open.

When appearance or interaction is hard to discuss abstractly, use
**preview-dashboard** to show a small number of distinct runnable options.
Synthetic values must be labeled. Let the learner choose and record what they
liked and what should change. Do not claim they approved an unseen result.

When the direction is clear, summarize it briefly and give the next sentence:
“ใช้ build-dashboard สร้างจากแบบที่เลือก แล้วเปิดให้ลอง”.
Do not automatically invoke build-dashboard; the learner may want to keep
exploring. If they explicitly requested both skills in their message, that
authorization already supplies the handoff.
