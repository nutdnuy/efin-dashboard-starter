The classroom adaptation exposes 4 user-invoked skills and 8 helpers. Eight
upstream user commands were consolidated into build-dashboard. All entry points
have simpler identifiers and Thai display names; workflows and configuration
templates were adapted for continuous delivery and local tool fallbacks.
The [name mapping](docs/skills.md) records the old and new names.
These are class adaptations, not all byte-for-byte upstream copies.

[skills.lock.json](skills.lock.json), schema version 2, records the current
SHA-256 of each distributed file, its upstream origin path(s) and original
hash(es), and whether its content is adapted or unchanged. Multiple origins
identify consolidated or derived content, not byte-identical copies. The
upstream revision remains pinned for provenance; the current manifest verifies
the classroom bundle. Renamed helper resources and the setup templates retain
their upstream attribution under the same license.
